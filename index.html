SUSTAINABLE AGRICULTURE & TERROIR

Biodiversity & Organic Sourcing Story

At EG Imports, LLC, we prioritize wineries that act as ecological sanctuaries. Our flagship partner estate, Dehesa de Luna, operates within a 3,000-hectare wildlife reserve where organic vines co-exist with imperial eagles, wild lavender, and centuries-old holm oaks.

  • Zero Chemical Pesticides: 100% certified organic cultivation with natural plant-based cover crops.
  • Vegan Fining Compliance: Clarified exclusively using pea protein and bentonite clay—zero animal by-products.
  • Water Harvesting & Renewable Energy: Solar-powered cellar processing and drip-irrigation sensor technology.

OUR CORE CAPABILITIES

Terroir & Services

CAPABILITY 01

Organic Terroir & Direct Import

At Euro Gourmet Imports, our portfolio is centered on 100% Eco-Certified organic wines. Native grape varieties, vineyard innovation, and environmental commitment are embedded in every single step we take.

Through exclusive direct supply agreements with premier Spanish estates—such as Dehesa de Luna, Sampayolo, and Bodegas Corcos & Izquierdo—we guarantee authentic origin purity, zero intermediary markups, and sustainable viticulture delivered straight to the Caribbean.

CAPABILITY 02

Overseas Cold-Chain Logistics

Fine organic wine requires uncompromised temperature care. From ancient Spanish cellars across maritime routes to the Caribbean, our products are shipped overseas in specialized 12°C–14°C reefer containers equipped with continuous thermal tracking.

We work tirelessly to safeguard the delicate character of every bottle, ensuring every vintage rests at its ideal recommended serving temperature before it arrives directly into our clients' hands.

CAPABILITY 03

Wine Tasting Presentations for Restaurants

We offer luxury restaurants, hotels, and fine dining venues the opportunity to schedule a private On-Site Wine Tasting Presentation led by our wine specialists.

CAPABILITY 04

Custom B2B Allocations

Pallet allocations, private cellar reservations, and bespoke co-bottling for resort chains, fine dining establishments, and cruise lines.
