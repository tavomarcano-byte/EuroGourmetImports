SUSTAINABLE AGRICULTURE & TERROIR

Biodiversity & Organic Sourcing Story

At EG Imports, LLC, we prioritize wineries that act as ecological sanctuaries. Our flagship partner estate, Dehesa de Luna, operates within a 3,000-hectare wildlife reserve where organic vines co-exist with imperial eagles, wild lavender, and centuries-old holm oaks.

  • Zero Chemical Pesticides: 100% certified organic cultivation with natural plant-based cover crops.
  • Vegan Fining Compliance: Clarified exclusively using pea protein and bentonite clay—zero animal by-products.
  • Water Harvesting & Renewable Energy: Solar-powered cellar processing and drip-irrigation sensor technology.

OUR COMMITMENT & SERVICES

Estate Sourcing & Trade Services

PILLAR 01

Centering on Organic Wines

Native varieties, vineyard innovation, and environmental commitment are at the heart of every single step we take.

At EG Imports, LLC, we partner exclusively with eco-certified estates that champion indigenous Spanish grapes—such as Godello, Mencía, Graciano, and bush-vine Tempranillo. By eliminating synthetic chemicals and embracing solar-powered cellars, we ensure every bottle reflects pure origin terroir while honoring the earth.

🌿 100% Eco-Certified Organic 🍇 Native Grapes & Heritage Vines ☀️ Solar-Powered Agriculture
PILLAR 02

Ocean Cold-Chain Integrity

From Spain’s stone cellars to Caribbean shores, we ensure every bottle travels under continuous climate perfection.

Cross-ocean voyages can compromise wine quality if exposed to heat. EG Imports, LLC utilizes 100% temperature-controlled reefer containers (locked at a precise 12°C–14°C / 54°F–57°F) equipped with continuous thermal tracking. Once landed in San Juan, bottles transition straight to our climate-controlled warehouse and white-glove reefer vehicles—guaranteeing every wine reaches your sommelier's hands in flawless cellar condition.

❄️ 12°C–14°C Constant Reefer Freight 📡 Real-Time Ocean Thermal Tracking 🍷 Insulated Cellar Delivery
