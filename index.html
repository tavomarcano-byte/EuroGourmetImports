“Great wines are not manufactured in factories; they are born from wild yeasts, ancient slate soils, and centuries of uncompromised family stewardship.”

Founded on the principle of direct, transparent estate imports, EG Imports (Euro Gourmet Imports) bridges the rich winemaking heritage of Spain directly to Puerto Rico and the Caribbean. We bypass speculative brokers to champion independent family producers who honor the land.

From solar-powered gravity wineries in Castilla-La Mancha to hand-harvested high-elevation bush vines in Valdeorras and Ribera del Duero, nuestro portafolio representa autenticidad pura, biodiversidad y excelencia artesanal.

Artisanal Spanish Gastronomy

D.O.P. Queso Manchego 100% Artesanal

Raw Sheep's Milk Aged Manchego

Crafted exclusively from 100% pure Manchega sheep's milk in Castilla-La Mancha. Cured naturally in stone cellars for 6 to 12 months, delivering a buttery, piquant flavor profile with characteristic zigzag esparto rind.

🍷 Sommelier Pairing Recommendation
Pair Curado Manchego with Dehesa de Luna Organic Red for a sublime balance of acidity and rich sheep's milk fats.
Jamón Ibérico 100% de Bellota

100% Acorn-Fed Iberian Ham

The undisputed pinnacle of Spanish gastronomy. Free-range 100% purebred Iberian pigs grazing on wild acorns across oak dehesas. Cured for 36+ months to yield sweet oleic acid-rich marbling that melts on the palate.

🍷 Sommelier Pairing Recommendation
Complement hand-carved Bellota slices with Sampayolo Godello or Corcos Selección for unmatched palate harmony.
